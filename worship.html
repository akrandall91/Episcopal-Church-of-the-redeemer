Sunday at Redeemer

Worship that carries us.

We gather to hear scripture, sing, pray for the world, receive a sermon, and share Holy Communion. Our worship is rooted in the Episcopal tradition and shaped by the lives, music, memory, and hope of this congregation.

Holy Communion

All baptized Christians are invited to receive Communion. If you prefer a blessing, cross your arms over your chest when you come forward.
