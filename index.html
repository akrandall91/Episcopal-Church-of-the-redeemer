Our Story

A Historic Parish With a Living Mission

Founded in 1909 as a mission for African-American Episcopalians, The Redeemer grew into a parish that has served generations of Greensboro families through worship, formation, fellowship, and community care.

Redeemer carries a legacy of faith, dignity, and community witness, including its life as a spiritual home and community anchor through the Civil Rights era. Its identity remains deeply Episcopal and connected to the wider Anglican Communion.

Today, multigenerational parish life brings elders, families, students, children, and community partners into one living story—rooted in faith and ready for the next generation.
