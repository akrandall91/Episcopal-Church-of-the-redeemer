Getting here

A warm, unhurried welcome

What to expect

What should I wear?

Wear what feels comfortable. You will see a range from Sunday dress to everyday clothes.

What happens in worship?

Our Episcopal worship includes scripture, prayer, music, a sermon, and Holy Communion. A printed bulletin guides every step. All baptized Christians are welcome to receive Communion; anyone may come forward for a blessing.

Where do I enter?

Look for welcome signs and greeters near the main entrance. Accessible entry and drop-off information is available on our Getting Here page.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Church School begins at 9:00 AM, and children are always welcome in worship. The sounds and movements of children belong in church.

What happens afterward?

Stay for fellowship, conversation, and a chance to meet people without pressure.
