What happens in an Episcopal service?

A sacred rhythm,
explained in plain language.

The service usually lasts about 75 minutes. The bulletin gives words, page numbers, and cues; watching and listening is always enough.

  1. 01

    We gather

    Music begins, the ministers enter, and we pray together. People may stand, sit, or kneel; follow what is comfortable for you.

  2. 02

    We hear the Word

    Readings from scripture, a sermon, the Creed, and prayers connect ancient faith with life now.

  3. 03

    We share peace

    We greet one another with a handshake, wave, or simple “peace.” You will not be singled out as a visitor.

  4. 04

    We share Communion

    All baptized Christians are welcome to receive bread and wine. Anyone may come forward for a blessing or remain seated.

  5. 05

    We are sent

    After the final hymn, we go into the world to love and serve. You are invited—but never pressured—to stay afterward.

Children, access & comfort

Bring your whole self—and your whole household.

Children’s voices and movement belong in worship. Church School begins at 9:00 AM, and children are always welcome in the sanctuary.

What should I wear?

Whatever helps you feel comfortable. You will see everything from suits and dresses to relaxed everyday clothing.

Will I have to introduce myself?

No. Greeters may welcome you, but visitors are not asked to stand or speak publicly.

What if I need mobility, hearing, or sensory support?

Tell us in the form below or call the office. We will identify the most helpful entrance, seating, and support available.

Can I come if I am unsure what I believe?

Yes. Curiosity, questions, and quiet presence are welcome here.
