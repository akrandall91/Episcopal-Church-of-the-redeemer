01

Serve on Sunday

Worship teams

Roles that help create a prayerful, welcoming service. Training is available, and several roles are open to youth and adults.

Request to serve

Choir

Lead congregational song each Sunday and offer music as ministry.

Music · Rehearsal

Acolytes

Carry the cross and torches, assist with the Gospel book, offering, and Eucharistic table. Youth age 8+ and adults are welcome.

Worship · Youth & adults

Lay readers

Proclaim scripture and, with additional clergy training, help lead portions of worship.

Speaking · Training

Ushers & greeters

Welcome newcomers, distribute bulletins, help with seating and Communion movement, and receive the offering.

Hospitality · Sunday

Altar Guild

Prepare vessels, linens, hangings, and the altar for each liturgical season and service.

Behind the scenes · Sacred care

Flowers

Help coordinate altar flowers offered to God in celebration, thanksgiving, or memory.

Beauty · Memorials

Fellowship, service, stewardship, reconciliation, and women’s participation in the life and governance of the church.

Spiritual and educational formation, fellowship, service, stewardship, and care for buildings and grounds.

Fellowship and community outreach for Redeemer’s youth.

04

Governance & administration

Parish leadership

These teams steward policy, finances, operations, and the long-term mission of the parish.

Clergy

The Rev. Michelle Roach serves as Rector, leading worship, pastoral care, teaching, and parish mission.

Vestry

Elected members set parish policies and goals, steward resources, and communicate about the church’s life and work.

Finance Committee

Supports canonical compliance and responsible stewardship of church money and property.

Policies & Procedures

Maintains practical guidance for routine operations, events, facilities, and unusual situations.
