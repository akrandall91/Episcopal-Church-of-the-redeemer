# Sacred Welcome / Living Legacy brand system

## Brand posture

Redeemer should feel light, hospitable, editorial, historic, Episcopal, and alive. The visual system holds institutional dignity without becoming cold, and warmth without becoming nostalgic or generic.

## Color

- Warm white: `#FAF8F3`
- Pure white: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft parchment: `#F2EDE3`
- Episcopal blue: `#0F6CB6`
- Deep Episcopal blue: `#073B70`
- Episcopal red: `#C61F3E`
- Warm gray: `#6D6D70`
- Deep charcoal: `#202124`
- Heritage gold: `#B9934A`
- Pale gold: `#EFE2C3`
- Soft blue tint: `#EAF2FB`

Blue is the primary interactive and structural accent. Red is reserved for Episcopal identity moments, live indicators, urgent emphasis, and small cross details. Gold appears as a fine highlight: rules, timeline dots, campaign recognition, and restrained hover details.

## Typography

- Headings: Newsreader, Georgia fallback
- Body and interface: DM Sans, Arial fallback

Use generous serif display sizes, readable sans-serif body copy, short line lengths, and a clear hierarchy. Avoid script fonts and all-caps body copy.

## Logo use

- Header: official Episcopal shield plus Redeemer wordmark text.
- Mobile/sticky header: shield remains visible and the church name stays readable.
- Footer: official horizontal Episcopal Church logo and a text line identifying the Diocese of North Carolina.
- Full-color logos belong on light backgrounds.
- Knockout files are only for dark or deep-blue backgrounds.
- Never recolor, distort, crop, or recreate the official Episcopal logo.

## Motion

Motion should resemble light entering a room: slow, quiet, and directional. Use opacity, transforms, reveal masks, route lines, and progress changes. All important content remains visible without JavaScript and animations stop under reduced-motion preferences.

## Photography

Favor documentary warmth, human presence, sacred details, and truthful architecture. Avoid stock-photo worship poses. Images should help visitors understand arrival, worship, people, legacy, restoration, and care.
