Privacy policy

Draft for church review. Last updated June 22, 2026.

Information we collect

When you submit a form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, preferences, requests, and consent choices.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond, coordinate pastoral or practical care, manage participation, communicate requested updates, and support church administration.

Sensitive requests

Prayer and pastoral care information should be limited to what is necessary. Access should be restricted to authorized church leaders or care team members.

Service providers

When configured, form data may be processed by Supabase and approved email, hosting, or administrative providers. Their privacy and security terms also apply.

Retention and choices

The church should adopt a written retention schedule. You may ask to review, correct, or delete your information, subject to pastoral, legal, financial, and operational requirements.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the church office at (336) 275-0033. This draft should be reviewed by church leadership and appropriate counsel before launch.
